LMN-3 for Windows
=================

Double-click LMN-3.exe. The first time, Windows SmartScreen may say
"Windows protected your PC" (the program isn't signed): click
"More info", then "Run anyway".

The computer keyboard and mouse stand in for the LMN-3's hardware.
Press F12 at any time to see the cheat sheet.

  The 24 keys      Z S X D C F V B H N J M   (lower octave, starting on F)
                   Q 2 W 3 E 4 R T 6 Y 7 U   (upper octave)
  Octave           Left / Right arrows
  Encoders         scroll the mouse wheel over a knob box at the bottom;
                   click a knob box to press it
                   or keys: 8 9 0 / I O P / K L ; / , . /
                   (down, up, press for encoders 1 to 4);
                   the Up/Down arrows also turn encoder 1
  Control          hold Shift
  Tabs             F1 Tracks  F2 Plugins  F3 Modifiers  F4 Sequencers
                   F5 Mixer   F6 Tempo    F7 Settings
  Transport        Space Play   Esc Stop   Enter Record
  Loop             [ Loop In   ] Loop Out   Ctrl+L Loop
  Plus / Minus     = (the +/= key)  and  -
  Save / Render    F8  /  Shift+F8
  Undo             Ctrl+Z
  Cut Paste Slice  Ctrl+X  Ctrl+V  Ctrl+D

The window can be made bigger by dragging its corner.

Your projects, samples and drum kits live in
  %APPDATA%\LMN-3
(paste that into the File Explorer address bar). Copy the contents of
your "samples" and "drum_kits" folders there, then restart LMN-3.

Sound goes to Windows' default output. Bluetooth headphones work but
add a noticeable delay; wired headphones or speakers feel much better.
