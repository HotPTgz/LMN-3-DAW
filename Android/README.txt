LMN-3 for Android
=================

Installing
  1. Copy LMN-3.apk to the phone (or download the zip on the phone and
     unzip it there), then tap it in the Files app.
  2. Android asks to allow installing apps from that app: allow it, go
     back and tap Install. If Play Protect warns that the app is unknown,
     tap "More details" and "Install anyway".
  3. To update later, install the new LMN-3.apk the same way: your
     projects stay.

Playing
  Hold the phone sideways.
  Keys            two octaves along the bottom, from F like the LMN-3;
                  slide a finger to play the keys it passes;
                  Oct - / Oct + change the octave
  Encoders        drag left/right (or up/down) on one of the four knob
                  boxes at the bottom of the screen; tap it to press it
  Ctrl            tap to turn it on, tap again to turn it off
  Big screen      hides the keys so the screen fills the height
                  (the same button, now called Keys, brings them back)
  Several fingers work at once: chords, or holding + while tapping a key.

Samples and drum kits
  Plug the phone into a computer (choose "File transfer" on the phone)
  and open
      Android/data/com.fundamentalfrequency.lmn3/files/LMN-3
  It holds the projects, renders, and the "samples" and "drum_kits"
  folders: copy your samples and kits there, then restart LMN-3.
  (The folder appears after LMN-3 has been opened once.)

Sound
  Wired headphones or a USB-C audio adapter respond fastest. Bluetooth
  headphones add a noticeable delay.

VST3 plugins don't exist on Android, so only the built-in instruments
and effects are available.
